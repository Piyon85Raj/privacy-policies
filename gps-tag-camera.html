Privacy Policy for GPS Tag Camera

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

GPS Tag Camera ("we", "our", or "the app") respects and values your privacy. This policy explains how our application handles permissions and data.

1. Permissions & Data Usage

  • Camera: Used exclusively to preview and capture photos within the application. Photos remain on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Location (Fine & Coarse): Accessed while using the app to retrieve your real-time coordinates, altitude, and street address to imprint watermark tags directly onto captured images. We do not track your location in the background or store/transmit your location data to any external server.
  • Storage & Gallery: Used strictly to save your tagged images to your device's photo gallery.

2. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The app may use third-party libraries (such as Google Play Services and Google AdMob) that collect non-personally identifiable identifiers solely for basic diagnostics, stability metrics, and serving ads in accordance with Google policies.

3. Data Security & Retention

All captured images and stamped geotags are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, transfer, or share your personal data with third parties.
