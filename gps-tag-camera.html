Privacy Policy for GPS Tag Camera

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

GPS Tag Camera ("we", "our", or "the app") values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle device permissions and data.

1. Permissions & Data Collection

  • Camera: Used strictly to preview and capture photos within the application. Images remain local to your device unless explicitly shared by you.
  • Location (Fine & Coarse): Accessed solely while using the camera to determine real-time coordinates, street address, and altitude to stamp metadata onto captured photos. We do not track location in the background, nor do we store or transmit your coordinates to any external server.
  • Photos / Storage: Used to save your tagged images directly to your device storage and photo gallery.

2. Third-Party Services

The app may use standard third-party developer libraries (such as Google Play Services and Google AdMob) that collect non-identifiable device identifiers strictly for performance diagnostics and ad delivery, in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent any personal user data. All photos and embedded GPS stamps are stored locally on your device.
